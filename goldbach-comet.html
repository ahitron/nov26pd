The Goldbach comet

Each dot is an even number n. Its height is the number of ways to write n as a sum of two primes. Hover, tap, or use the arrow keys to see the sums.

Why are there bands?

Take an even n that is a multiple of 3, and a prime p other than 3. Then n − p is not a multiple of 3 either, so p and n − p can both be prime.

If n is 1 or 2 more than a multiple of 3, half of the primes p fail. For example, if n ≡ 1 (mod 3) and p ≡ 1 (mod 3), then n − p is a multiple of 3. So multiples of 3 get about twice as many chances.

The same idea with 5 gives a smaller boost: about 4/3 times as many ways for multiples of 5.
